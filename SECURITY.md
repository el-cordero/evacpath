# Security Policy

Please do not report potential security or privacy vulnerabilities in a
public issue. Instead, email <elvin.cordero@seamountgeo.com> with a
concise description, the affected package version, and steps to
reproduce the concern.

The maintainer will acknowledge the report, assess the impact, and
coordinate a fix or public disclosure as appropriate. This policy also
applies to accidental exposure of sensitive spatial, infrastructure, or
evacuation-planning data in project materials.
