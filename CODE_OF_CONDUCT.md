# Code of Conduct

`evacpath` welcomes participation from people with different backgrounds,
disciplines, and levels of experience. We are committed to a respectful,
constructive environment for discussions, issues, pull requests, and other
project spaces.

Expected behavior includes using welcoming language, accepting constructive
feedback, focusing on evidence and the work, and respecting differing
perspectives. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and deliberate
disruption are not acceptable.

To report a concern, contact the maintainer at
elvin.cordero@seamountgeo.com. Reports will be handled as privately as the
circumstances allow.
